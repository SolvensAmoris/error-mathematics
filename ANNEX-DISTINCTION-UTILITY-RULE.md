## Rule

> Cada distinción conceptual debe justificar su utilidad mediante una consecuencia observable, matemática o experimental que permita diferenciarla de las alternativas.

A distinction that cannot be differentiated from its alternatives by any such consequence does not yet earn its place in the working vocabulary, however elegant its definition.

This is stricter than coherence of vocabulary. The program does not need to demonstrate that its vocabulary is coherent; it needs to demonstrate that the vocabulary allows distinguishing phenomena that would otherwise remain confused.

## Relation to existing principles

- P3 already separates error, failure, and uncertainty; this rule adds the test for whether that separation does explanatory work.
- P4 already requires invariance to name its transformation class; this rule adds the test for whether the claimed invariance survives a change of measurement conditions, and if not, its domain must be delimited or the claim rejected.
- P8 already forbids forced cross-domain equivalence; this rule adds the test for Da Vinci Bridge connectors: a correspondence must be demonstrated, not asserted by analogy.
- P10 already separates exploration from confirmation; this rule applies the same discipline to the vocabulary itself.

## Operational tests

1. **ERROR vs FAILURE:** If the two always produce the same classification, the distinction adds no explanatory capacity. A useful distinction requires cases that separate them — error without failure, and failure without error — as already anticipated in P3.
2. **INVARIANCE:** If a supposed invariance disappears when measurement conditions change, delimit its domain or reject it. State what changes, what remains, and within which limits (P4).
3. **RELATIONAL_TRANSFORMATION:** If it does not allow anticipating observations distinct from those already predicted by a conventional model, it does not yet add explanatory power.
4. **Da Vinci Bridge:** If a bridge needs analogies to connect two mathematical structures but cannot demonstrate their correspondences — trunks independently justified, connectors explicit, compatibility conditions stated — the bridge is not built.

## Scope: MIOD-001

MIOD-001 must not carry the obligation to validate the whole program. It is a particular experiment.

It can provide evidence, limit a hypothesis, or refute one, without that confirming or destroying the other lines automatically. Local evidence has local scope; any wider consequence must be argued explicitly, not inherited by association.

## Next step (unchanged)

Operationalize what "reorganización" means in the water system — observable, threshold, persistence, recovery, and what counts as a new organization versus a transient pattern — before confirmatory trials (protocol §5, §7). That is where the conceptual framework first confronts the physical world.

## Separation of evidence

- Vocabulary coherence is not evidence.
- A distinction is not a result.
- A negative result is evidence (README commitment 5; P6), including a distinction that fails its utility test.
