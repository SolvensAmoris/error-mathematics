# H003 — Observation Conditions Contribute to Measured Discrepancy

**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Version:** 0.1

## Statement
For at least some systems, measured discrepancies depend in a measurable way on the observer, instrument, calibration, sampling, or processing procedure.

## Scope
This is not a claim that reality is created by observation. It is a testable claim that measurement outcomes can depend on the measurement arrangement.

## Predictions
Controlled changes in observation conditions may produce systematic differences in measured quantities or inferred relational structure. These differences should be characterizable through calibration, cross-instrument comparison, blinded analysis, or other appropriate controls.

## Competing explanations
Genuine changes in the physical system between measurements; environmental drift; non-equivalent sampling; software or processing defects; observer expectation or selection effects.

## Rejection or limitation
The hypothesis is unsupported for a tested measurement if controlled observation changes do not produce detectable differences within the sensitivity and uncertainty of the procedure.

## Evidence
No confirmatory evidence registered yet.
