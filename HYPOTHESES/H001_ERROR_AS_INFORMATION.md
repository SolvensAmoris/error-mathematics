# H001 — Structured Error May Contain Information

**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Version:** 0.1

## Statement
Under some specified conditions, discrepancies between expected and observed behavior contain reproducible structure that provides information not captured by a model focused only on expected or functional behavior.

## Scope
This is not a claim that all errors are informative, that all anomalies are real, or that error is always intrinsic to the system.

## Operational interpretation
1. Define the reference, prediction, or comparison.
2. Record observations and measurement conditions.
3. Calculate or classify discrepancy using a declared procedure.
4. Test whether discrepancy structure depends on controlled variables.
5. Evaluate whether it improves explanation or prediction on independent observations.

## Predictions
If supported in a given domain, some discrepancy patterns will recur under specified conditions, be distinguishable from measurement artifacts and plausible noise models, and provide explanatory or predictive information beyond the baseline model on independent data.

## Competing explanations
Random variation; instrument drift or calibration error; observer or selection bias; unmeasured confounding; data-processing artifacts; overfitting or post hoc pattern selection.

## Rejection or limitation
The hypothesis is unsupported for a tested domain if apparent structure fails replication, disappears under appropriate controls, or provides no independent explanatory or predictive value beyond plausible alternatives.

## Evidence
No confirmatory evidence registered yet.
