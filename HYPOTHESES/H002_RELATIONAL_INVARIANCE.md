# H002 — Relational Invariance Across Distinct Systems

**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Version:** 0.1

## Statement
Two physically distinct systems may exhibit a common, nontrivial mathematical relation describing how specified relations transform under interaction, even when their physical forms and observables differ.

## Scope
This hypothesis does not assert that all interacting systems share one law. It requires a precise mapping between operationally defined variables in each domain.

## Predictions
A candidate common relation should be defined before cross-domain comparison, generate discriminating predictions in each domain, remain robust within declared uncertainty and conditions, and withstand plausible alternative descriptions.

## Competing explanations
Superficial analogy; overly flexible mappings; shared generic dynamics with no distinctive predictive value; domain-specific mechanisms that only appear equivalent under coarse measurement.

## Rejection or limitation
Reject the proposed cross-domain equivalence if the mapping cannot be operationalized, fails independent tests, or has no predictive advantage over trivial or domain-specific descriptions.
