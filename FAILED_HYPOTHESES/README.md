# Failed and Superseded Hypotheses

This directory preserves hypotheses that were rejected, unsupported, contradicted, or superseded.

For every entry, record the original statement and version, reason for testing, evidence and protocol, what failed and what did not, alternative explanations, whether the failure concerns hypothesis, measurement, apparatus, or analysis, and follow-up questions.

Do not rewrite historical hypotheses to make them appear successful. Negative results and methodological failures are part of the research record.
